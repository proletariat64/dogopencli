Observed: 2026-10-02
Scope: general
Status: snapshot

Date: 2026-10-02. Specification: [#76](https://github.com/proletariat64/dogopencli/issues/76).

This report records field names and observed types after the accepted plugin
projections. No account identifiers, record values, cookies or tokens are
included. Source samples are the explicit inquiry responses described by the
[Cash / Forecast business probe](2026-10-02-cash-forecast-business-probe.md).

Projection samples describe returned fields, not completeness guarantees or
nullable/type promises for all production data. Fixed scope annotations such as
`equiCurrency=USD` are request-bound metadata, not invented source observations.
Unknown native observation times remain unknown. Settings remove runtime amounts;
independent native pool detail evidence is distinct from list evidence.
